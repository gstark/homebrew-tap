# gstark/homebrew-tap

This is a personal Homebrew tap. It holds formulas and casks for my own projects and personal builds.

## Warning

I do not recommend this tap for other people.

- The packages are for my own use.
- The packages can change or break at any time.
- I do not give support for these packages.
- I do not keep stable versions.

## Contents

- `Formula/`: Homebrew formulas.
- `Casks/`: Homebrew casks.
